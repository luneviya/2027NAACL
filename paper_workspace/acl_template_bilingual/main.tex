\title{\method{}: Learning from Retriever-Induced Confusions for Code Search}

% Keep the submission anonymous while using the review option.

\begin{abstract}
Neural code search systems commonly optimize static hard negatives that need not match the high-ranked confusions produced at inference time. We propose \method{}, a failure-calibrated refinement framework that mines high-ranked non-ground-truth candidates from a frozen retriever and uses them as targeted contrastive supervision. \method{} first refines global query--code representations and then applies a parameter-free token-level MaxSim reranker only to the top-\(K\) candidates. Under a matched local protocol on the six-language CodeSearchNet benchmark, refinement raises macro MRR from 79.8 to 80.6, and final reranking reaches 81.1, a 1.3-point paired improvement over the initial retriever with a query-level 95\% bootstrap confidence interval of [1.11, 1.47]. Candidate-selection controls and rank-stratified diagnostics show that the gains concentrate on initially mis-ranked queries, while also revealing regressions for some initial top-1 cases.
% 中文：神经代码检索系统通常使用静态困难负样本进行优化，但这些负样本未必对应模型在推理时实际产生的高排名混淆候选。本文提出 \method{}，一种失败校准的细化框架：它从冻结的检索器中挖掘高排名非标注候选，并将其作为有针对性的对比监督。\method{} 首先细化全局查询—代码表示，随后仅对前 \(K\) 个候选应用无参数的词元级 MaxSim 重排器。在六语言 CodeSearchNet 基准上的协议一致本地评测中，全局细化将宏平均 MRR 从 79.8 提升到 80.6，最终重排达到 81.1；相比初始检索器配对提高 1.3 个点，查询级 95\% bootstrap 置信区间为 [1.11, 1.47]。候选选择对照和分层排名诊断表明，收益主要集中在初始排序错误的查询上，同时也揭示了部分初始首位样本发生回退。
\end{abstract}

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate \method{} from three complementary perspectives. First, we compare its retrieval effectiveness with representative code-search systems, a matched initial retriever, and alternative encoder backbones. Second, budget-matched candidate controls and a crossed training--inference comparison isolate the contribution of retriever-induced candidate selection from continued optimization and generic top-\(K\) reranking. Third, paired uncertainty estimates, rank-conditioned analyses, and latency and memory measurements characterize the reliability, distribution, and computational cost of the observed improvements.
% 中文：我们从三个相互补充的角度评估 \method{}。首先，将其检索效果与具有代表性的代码搜索系统、协议一致的初始检索器以及不同编码器骨干进行比较。其次，通过预算一致的候选对照和训练—推理交叉比较，区分检索器诱导候选选择的贡献、继续优化的影响以及通用前 \(K\) 重排带来的收益。最后，结合配对不确定性估计、按初始排名分层的分析以及延迟和显存测量，刻画性能提升的可靠性、分布特征和计算成本。

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}

\paragraph{Implementation and controlled comparisons.}
The primary configuration uses the CoCoSoDa~\citep{CoCoSoDa} encoder and follows the UA-HN~\citep{AAAIUA} training protocol. The initial retriever and both \method{} variants share the same data partitions, candidate codebases, and evaluation implementation. Candidate-selection controls hold the refinement schedule and added-candidate budget fixed while varying only the candidate construction rule: a uniformly sampled non-positive (Random), a fixed pre-mined hard negative (Static), or a high-ranked non-ground-truth candidate produced by the frozen retriever (FC). This design separates the effect of retriever-conditioned supervision from the effect of continued optimization under the examined budget.
% 中文：主要实验配置采用 CoCoSoDa~\citep{CoCoSoDa} 编码器，并遵循 UA-HN~\citep{AAAIUA} 的训练协议。初始检索器与两个 \method{} 变体使用相同的数据划分、候选代码库和评测实现。候选选择对照固定细化训练日程和新增候选预算，仅改变候选构造规则：均匀采样的非正例（Random）、固定的预挖掘困难负例（Static），或由冻结检索器产生的高排名非标注候选（FC）。该设计用于区分检索器条件监督的作用与相同预算下继续优化本身的影响。

\paragraph{Training and model selection.}
The initial retriever is trained for 10 epochs with batch size 128, learning rate \(8\times10^{-6}\), and temperature 0.03. FC supervision is constructed by freezing this checkpoint, retrieving over the training codebase, removing the paired code and same-URL candidates, and retaining the 32 highest-ranked remaining candidates. Refinement considers the first eight candidates and samples one at each update. The refinement stage runs for three epochs with batch size 128, learning rate \(5\times10^{-6}\), FC weight 0.45, and FC temperature 0.03. Its objective operates on global query--code representations; no CTRD or token-level local-interaction loss is used during training.
% 中文：初始检索器训练 10 个 epoch，批量大小为 128，学习率为 \(8\times10^{-6}\)，温度为 0.03。构造 FC 监督时，我们冻结该检查点，在训练代码库上执行检索，排除配对代码以及 URL 相同的候选，并保留排名最高的 32 个剩余候选。细化训练考虑其中前 8 个候选，并在每次更新时采样 1 个。细化阶段训练 3 个 epoch，批量大小为 128，学习率为 \(5\times10^{-6}\)，FC 权重为 0.45，FC 温度为 0.03。其目标作用于全局查询—代码表示；训练过程中不使用 CTRD 或词元级局部交互损失。

At inference time, \method{}-Global preserves dual-encoder retrieval over the full codebase. The optional reranking stage computes a parameter-free token-level MaxSim score only for the global top-\(K\) candidates and fuses query-wise standardized global and local scores. Six-language validation macro MRR selects \(K=20\) and fusion weight \(\alpha=0.5\); both values are frozen before test evaluation. Model checkpoints are likewise selected exclusively on validation data. All local results use training seed 123456 and one NVIDIA H20 GPU.
% 中文：在推理阶段，\method{}-Global 保持对完整代码库的双编码器检索。可选重排阶段只对全局检索得到的前 \(K\) 个候选计算无需训练参数的词元级 MaxSim 分数，并融合按查询标准化后的全局分数与局部分数。我们依据六种语言验证集上的宏平均 MRR 选择 \(K=20\) 和融合权重 \(\alpha=0.5\)，并在测试前冻结二者。模型检查点同样只根据验证集选择。所有本地结果均使用训练种子 123456，并在一张 NVIDIA H20 GPU 上运行。

\paragraph{Uncertainty estimates.}
We use a stratified paired bootstrap over test queries to characterize query-sampling uncertainty in the difference between each refined system and the initial retriever. These intervals condition on the single trained checkpoint and therefore do not measure variation across training seeds.
% 中文：我们在测试查询上采用分层配对 bootstrap，刻画细化系统与初始检索器之间差值的查询采样不确定性。这些区间以单个已训练检查点为条件，因此不能衡量不同训练种子带来的波动。

\paragraph{Result provenance.}
We distinguish published and locally evaluated results throughout the experiments. Rows marked with \reported{} reproduce values from the cited papers and situate \method{} relative to prior work; rows marked with \localrun{} are obtained with our evaluation pipeline. Effect estimates and paired confidence intervals are computed only between locally evaluated systems that share the same protocol.
% 中文：我们在整个实验部分明确区分论文报告结果和本地评测结果。标记为 \reported{} 的行复用所引用论文中的数值，用于说明 \method{} 相对于既有工作的表现位置；标记为 \localrun{} 的行由本文评测流程得到。效应差值和配对置信区间只在使用相同协议的本地评测系统之间计算。

\subsection{Controlled Effect of Candidate Selection}
\label{sec:candidate_controls}

Table~\ref{tab:candidate_controls} isolates candidate construction under an equal budget of one added non-ground-truth candidate per training query. The Random, Static HN, and retriever-mined FC conditions use the same refinement schedule and global-retrieval evaluation. Random and Static HN do not exceed the unrefined checkpoint, whereas FC increases macro MRR by 0.35 points and R@1 by 0.83 points. Under this matched budget, candidates drawn from the retriever's own high-ranked neighborhood provide a more informative refinement signal than the two alternative construction rules.

\subsection{Rank-Conditioned Retrieval Behavior}
\label{sec:rank_analysis}

% 中文：我们按照细化前标注代码的排名，对全部 52,561 个测试查询进行分层。表~\ref{tab:rank_strata} 显示，最大收益出现在初始排名第 2--10 位：\method{}-Global 使该分层的 MRR 提升 8.89 个点，加入重排后提升扩大到 11.32 个点。第 11--50 位和 50 位以后的变化较小但仍为正。提升集中在高排名邻域，这与 \method{} 所使用的训练信号相一致。

Rank transitions expose behavior that is not visible in the aggregate MRR. Relative to the initial ranking, \method{}-Rerank promotes the labeled code for 7,121 queries and demotes it for 5,741. It moves 2,644 non-top-1 cases to rank 1, while 1,624 initially top-ranked cases move downward; MRR in the initial-rank-1 stratum decreases by 2.47 points. \subsection{Pipeline Integrity and Provenance}
\label{sec:audit}

We audit split isolation, candidate caches, checkpoint lineage, model selection, and test invocation. Of 121 automated checks, 115 pass and none produces a critical failure. Exact URL, repository, and normalized-code overlap across training, validation, and test partitions is zero, and labeled-code coverage in the validation and test codebases is 100\%. Every FC cache contains 32 unique, in-range, training-only candidates per query and excludes the paired example and same-URL code. Independent reconstruction of validation-based model selection recovers \(K=20,\alpha=0.5\).
% 中文：我们审计了数据划分隔离、候选缓存、检查点来源、模型选择和测试调用。在 121 项自动检查中，115 项通过，且没有关键失败。训练集、验证集和测试集之间的 URL、代码仓库及规范化代码精确重叠均为零，验证集和测试集代码库中的标注代码覆盖率为 100\%。每个 FC 缓存对每个查询都包含 32 个唯一、索引有效且仅来自训练集的候选，并排除配对样本和 URL 相同的代码。独立重建基于验证集的模型选择过程后，同样得到 \(K=20,\alpha=0.5\)。

The six warnings correspond to natural-language strings duplicated in the frozen upstream benchmark partitions. We retain and disclose these instances without altering the benchmark after inspection. The audit verifies the integrity and provenance of the executed pipeline; semantic relevance of unpaired candidates is considered separately in the limitations.
% 中文：六条警告来自冻结的上游基准划分中重复出现的自然语言文本。我们保留并披露这些样本，不在检查数据后修改基准。该审计验证了实际执行流程的完整性和来源可追溯性；未配对候选的语义相关性问题则在局限性部分单独讨论。

\section*{Limitations}

Our evidence is limited to CodeSearchNet, one audited training seed, and the completed primary encoder configuration. Query-level bootstrap intervals quantify evaluation-set uncertainty but do not establish stability across independent training runs. Until the UA-HN MaxSim control is completed, the refinement-specific evidence is provided by the global-retrieval comparison, while the final reranked score should be interpreted as the performance of the combined system. CodeSearchNet also provides only one labeled code snippet per query, so some high-ranked non-ground-truth candidates may be valid alternative implementations; their prevalence cannot be quantified until the reserved annotation study is completed. Finally, the parameter-free reranker adds latency over global retrieval, and the pending transfer study does not yet establish backbone independence.
% 中文：当前证据仅覆盖 CodeSearchNet、一个经过审计的训练随机种子，以及已经完成的主要编码器配置。查询级 bootstrap 区间能够刻画评测集不确定性，但不能证明不同独立训练检查点之间的稳定性。在 UA-HN MaxSim 对照完成之前，细化本身的证据来自全局检索条件下的比较，而最终重排分数应解释为组合系统的表现。CodeSearchNet 每个查询只提供一个标注代码，因此部分高排名非标注候选可能是有效替代实现；在预留的人工标注研究完成前，无法量化其占比。此外，无参数重排器会在全局检索之上增加延迟，尚未完成的迁移研究目前也不能证明骨干模型无关性。
